Cyber Security is a shared responsibility
itis How to protect systems, networks, programs,
devices and data from any attacks

Security Goals: (C . I . A)
	1-Confidentiality:
		information should only be seen by authorized people.
		Confidentiality = Keep data secret.
	2. Integrity:
		Information should remain correct and unchanged unless an authorized modification is made. Any unauthorized change should be detectable.
		Integrity = Keep data accurate and trustworthy.
	3-Availability
		Information and resources should be accessible to authorized users whenever they need them.
		Availability = Keep data and services accessible.


Security deals with managing risk to your critical assets
	Risk = Threat x Vulnerabilities x Impact
	Vulnerability is the degree of weakness which is found in every network and device.
	Threats is A person, thing, event or idea which poses danger to an asset in terms of that asset’s confidentiality,
	integrity, availability or legitimate use

Attackers Types:
	Black hats (Offensively)
		• These are cybercriminals who exploit system vulnerabilities
	 White Hats (Defensively)
		• they are authorized professionals who test systems to identify and fix security flaws.
	Gray Hats (Offensively) (Defensively)
		• Operate without permission but usually without malicious intent.

Passive attack VS Active attack
	Passive attack attempts to take the information
	from the system and does not affect any system
	resources and its operations.
		▪ Ex : Cookies , Spyware , Wireshark
	Active attack attempts to change the system
	resources or affect their usual operations.
		▪ Ex : Ransomware, Viruses, worms

PHISHING ATTACK:
	the hacker creates a fake web site that looks exactly like a popular site.

BUFFER OVERFLOW ATTACK:
	when the attacker sends
	more data to an application than is expected.
